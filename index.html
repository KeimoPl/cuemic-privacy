Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026 · Published by KEMO

CueMic is a Windows soundboard for playing clips and adding them to your calls. This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it.

Information stored on your PC

CueMic does not require an account. Your imported sound files, sound names, cover images you upload, collections, and app preferences are stored locally on your PC. CueMic does not send this library or these preferences to KEMO.

Microphone and audio

When you choose Start mic, CueMic uses your selected microphone to pass live audio to the playback and call audio devices you have selected. CueMic does not record or save microphone audio. Sound clips are played to the output devices you select.

If you route audio to a virtual cable and use it in a calling, streaming, or recording app, that other app handles the audio under its own settings and privacy policy. Windows may also show its own microphone permission and activity indicators.

Cover image links

You can use an HTTPS link for a sound's cover image. CueMic loads that image directly from the website hosting it. That website may receive your IP address and ordinary connection information, and its own privacy policy applies. Uploaded cover images remain on your PC and are not sent to CueMic's publisher.

Virtual audio driver

CueMic can install the bundled VB-Audio VB-CABLE driver to provide virtual call audio. Driver installation is an operating-system action. VB-CABLE is third-party software; its publisher's terms and privacy practices apply to that component.

Data collection and sharing

CueMic does not include advertising, analytics, an online account, or a CueMic service that collects or sells your personal information. The app does not send your sound library or microphone audio to KEMO.

The Microsoft Store, Windows, your network provider, linked-image websites, and other apps you choose to use operate under their own policies.

Your choices and deletion

You control the sound files and preferences stored on your PC. You can remove sounds in CueMic and open the local sound-library folder from Settings. Uninstalling CueMic does not necessarily remove its local app data; after uninstalling, you can delete that data from Windows if you want to remove the remaining library and preferences.

Policy changes

If CueMic's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change is included in a release. The date above shows when this policy was last revised.
